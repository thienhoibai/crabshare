# CrabShare investor screens

Recorded 2026-11-18 for the user-authorized investor UX improvement.

Keep the existing logo, Bricolage Grotesque, orange accent and warm neutral tokens. Scope layout and control overrides to `.o2--investor`; other roles retain their styles. The shell remains shared.

Lead with investor actions and their actual deadlines. Capital is a compact ledger with contributed, disbursed and held amounts. Label its frozen-batch scope explicitly and show confirmed contributions awaiting capital close separately. A failed source must not look like an empty queue.

Keep the existing five portfolio tabs and selected URLs. Operational progress and alerts are visible without burying the tabs below large cards. Mobile financial records restack while retaining every field and destination.

Use sentence case labels, plain metadata, flat stat rows, one primary action style, visible keyboard focus and reduced motion. No display eyebrows, decorative status dots or new illustrative assets. Do not infer missing financial, voting or opening thresholds.

Payment receipt, complete signatures, confirmed contribution, published statement, approved amount and successful payout are separate states. Use current API fields rather than visual step completion as evidence.
